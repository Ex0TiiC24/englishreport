📑 Prompt Specifications & Business Rules

Rules applied to both AiPASS and Native Gemini implementations

🛒 1. E-Commerce Store

  • Catalog: 6 items in Gadgets, Accessories, Audio (400, 600, 1200, 1500, 2200, 3000 THB).
  • Discount: If Subtotal ≥ 2,000 THB → 15% discount (Subtotal * 0.15), else 0.00.
  • Tax / VAT: 7% VAT computed on net subtotal: ((Subtotal - Discount) * 0.07).
  • Grand Total: (Subtotal - Discount) + Tax.

📚 2. Book Store System

  • Catalog: 6 books in Fiction, Tech, Business (180, 220, 280, 320, 450, 550 THB).
  • Discount: If Total Book Quantity ≥ 3 → flat 80.00 THB discount, else 0.00.
  • Shipping Fee (in #tax-val): If Cart ≥ 1 book → flat 50.00 THB, else 0.00.
  • Grand Total: max(0, Subtotal - Discount) + Shipping Fee.

🍜 3. Food Store / POS System

  • Catalog: 6 dishes in Main, Drink, Dessert (60, 70, 90, 110, 120, 180 THB).
  • Discount: If Subtotal ≥ 300 THB → flat 40.00 THB discount, else 0.00.
  • Service Charge (in #tax-val): 10% computed on net subtotal: ((Subtotal - Discount) * 0.10).
  • Grand Total: (Subtotal - Discount) + Service Charge.

💾 Persistence & Validation Standards (Module 5)

Both implementations write live cart state to localStorage.getItem("app_cart_state"), persist across page reloads, block order checkout when empty or missing customer name, and commit confirmed orders to localStorage.getItem("app_last_order").
